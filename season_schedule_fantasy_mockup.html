📅 Season Schedule — fantasy lens

NBA schedule read through this league's rules: 822-game cap (82 per PG/SG/SF/PF/C/G/F, 248 UTIL), 10 active slots, daily lineups, season-long roto.
Why this page is not just a schedule. With a games cap, more NBA games is not automatically more stats — your ceiling is 822 slot-games, not 82×13. The schedule only matters where it makes cap unreachable (too few players playing on a given night) or wasted (more players than slots, so a game gets burned on your worst option). Every view here answers one of those two.

Reuses team_slot_pace, SLOT_CAPS, NbaMatchupService — no new data source beyond the forward schedule.
