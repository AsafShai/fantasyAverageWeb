Correction: Standings Race is not standalone

I told you it was built-but-unrouted. Wrong — Analytics.tsx:6 imports it and renders it at line 372 as the Rankings Over Time tab. Nothing is stranded. I should have grepped for imports before saying it.
But that finding answers your naming question better than a rename would. Analytics has no content of its own — it's a tab host for Heatmap, Rankings Over Time (the Standings Race component) and Shooting Stats. That's why "League Analytics" reads badly as a nav label: it's a folder wearing a page's name.

So don't rename it — promote it. Analytics becomes the group; its three tabs become the entries. They already have real names, so nothing needs inventing, and Player Trends gets obvious company.

2. Where does Trade go?

Trade Analyzer: Tools group League Players
Why it won't settle: it's the only workspace in the app. Every other page shows something that exists (Teams, Players, Injuries) or something a model produced (Projections, Estimator). Trade is the one place you assemble something that doesn't exist yet and evaluate it.

A category of one is a bad group — but it isn't one for long. Streaming Planner and Slot Estimator are the same kind of page: assemble a hypothetical move, see what it does. That's three, and it's why a Tools group is defensible here when it wasn't before.
The discipline that makes Tools survivable this time: it means workspaces where you assemble a hypothetical move — not "things that feel like tools". It rejects Estimator (output, nothing to assemble), Player Rankings (weights, but no hypothetical roster) and Projections (model output). Those three are exactly what got dumped in last time. Write the rejections in the doc or it rots again.
Tools group name: 🛠️ Tools 🔄 Moves 🗓️ Planning 🧰 Workshop

model pages: Home to their domain Own Forecast group

Home puts Estimator in League (it predicts your final roto rank) and Projections in Players (it predicts a player's line). That kills the Forecast group whose name you didn't like, and both land somewhere obvious. Own group keeps them together because they're model output rather than recorded fact — a real distinction, but one that only matters if you think of them as "the model's pages" first.
