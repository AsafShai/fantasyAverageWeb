Round 4 — the strip, made fluid and self-labelling

Fixing your three points: nothing is a fixed pixel width (slider is flex:1, the values are an auto-fit grid that redistributes at any width), every variant says it's a projection, and each is rendered at three real container widths so you can see the mobile reflow instead of trusting a media query. Real data: 13.7 / 3.8 / 4.2 at his default 31 minutes.
