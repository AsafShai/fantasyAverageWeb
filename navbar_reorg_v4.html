Why "Tools" rotted, and why this axis doesn't

The test that kills "Tools": if the predicate is "page has inputs and computes something", it swallows Player Rankings (weights + Calculate button) and Trends (baseline picker). Both are analysis of past production. That's exactly how the drawer became a dumping ground last time.
