Slot cap bar — rebuilt anatomy

Your questions were all symptoms of one mistake: I layered four quantities plus a reference marker into one strip, using two identical black lines and a floating red block, then explained it in a legend. Redone so the bar is one continuous left-to-right story — every boundary is a real number, nothing floats, and only one thing is a marker.

Your questions, answered on the bar

"Why is the unreachable part after the cap?"
It isn't — and that was the bug in the old design. The cap is the whole width of the bar, not a line inside it. The bar always spans 0 → cap, so red sits inside the cap, at the far end. The bracket above the diagram now says this out loud instead of leaving you to infer it.
"There are two lines and I can't tell them apart."
Now there's one. The est. tick is gone: est is where colour 2 ends, so the boundary carries the value instead of a line drawn on top of it. Pace stays a marker because it's the only quantity that isn't a segment boundary — it's an outside reference, so it gets its own shape and its own hue.
"What is cap remaining?"
Deleted. It was one grey area covering two different things — cap you can still get, and cap you can't. Those are now separate colours with separate names: still possible and gone.
"What does the red mean?"
Dead cap. With 22 game days left, a 1-per-day slot can add at most 22 more — so C tops out at 74 of 82 and those last 8 are unreachable. Red only appears when a slot is in that state; most slots have no red at all.

All eight slots

hover any segment
Hover on desktop, chips on mobile. Every segment carries its own tooltip naming the quantity, its value, and what it means — so the legend is a reminder, not a prerequisite. Touch devices can't hover, so the same four values stay printed as chips under every bar, colour-keyed to their segment. Nothing is hover-only.

Read the roster in one pass: only C and F carry red, so those are the two slots with cap you've already lost. C is also the only bar whose purple marker sits far to the right of the blue — behind pace and not projected to catch up. SG, G and UTIL have blue running past purple: ahead of pace.
