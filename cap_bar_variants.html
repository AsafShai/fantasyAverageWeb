Why it wins: reads identically at both widths — chips wrap instead of shrinking. Two lines per slot × 8 = 16 lines, still shorter than the current 3-row table on mobile. The unreachable red block is the one thing you actually act on and it is impossible to miss.

Trade-off: best number-scanning of the four — aligned columns beat chips when comparing slots. But at 375px the bar column is squeezed to ~70px and stops communicating proportion, so you are really just reading a table. Viable if you drop the bar below sm.

C · Today's table, bar as cell background

Smallest possible change to SlotUsageTable.tsx.

Trade-off: keeps every number and the layout you already ship — the fill just replaces the tone class. But a fill inside an 8-column grid is 40px wide on mobile; proportion is unreadable, so it buys almost nothing over the current colour coding. Pick this only if the goal is "don't touch the layout".

Why it fails: most striking on desktop — the C column visibly falls short of everything else. But 8 columns in 375px is ~40px each, so the three numbers stack into an unreadable pile and UTIL's 248 cap makes every other column look full at a glance. Would need horizontal scroll, which defeats the point of a summary.
One thing all four fix. The current table shows Max as a number (74/82) — you have to subtract to notice C is already dead. Every variant here shows it as territory the bar can never enter, so "8 games of C cap are gone" is visual, not arithmetic.

Note on UTIL. Its cap is 248 and it gains up to 3/day, so on a shared axis it always looks proportionally similar to the rest. Each bar is scaled to its own cap — comparison across slots is by fill fraction, not width. Worth an InfoTip.
