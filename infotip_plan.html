The problem

Broken on touch

Native title= never fires on mobile

Trends.tsx has 14 good header tooltips. Phone users see none of them. Most of the league is on a phone.

Missing entirely

z-score pages explain nothing

Rankings, PlayerRankings, Teams — zero title=. "z: 1.84" with no scale, no baseline, no hint it's good.

Fix

One <InfoTip> component

Visible ⓘ affordance, click/tap on both platforms, styled, dark-mode aware. Then cover the blind pages.

Behaviour spec

Desktop

  • ⓘ is always visible — discoverable, unlike a bare hover zone.
  • Hover opens after 120ms; leave closes after 100ms grace.
  • Click pins open. Esc or outside-click closes.
  • Bubble anchored under the header, flips above / shifts left near viewport edges.

Mobile (≈390px)

  • No hover. Tap only.
  • Hit target 40×40 via invisible padding — the glyph stays 14px so dense headers don't grow.
  • Bubble becomes a bottom sheet, full width, with an ✕ — never clipped by overflow-x-auto on the table.
  • Tap outside or ✕ closes. One open at a time.

Why bottom sheet on mobile: your wide tables live inside overflow-x-auto. An absolutely-positioned bubble gets clipped or forces horizontal scroll. Fixed-position sheet sidesteps it entirely.
