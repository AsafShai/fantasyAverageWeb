Navbar Reorg — final structure

Five groups, everything two clicks, no flat links.

Changed from the previous round. Analytics and Forecast merged into one Insights group — both were "you look, you don't do", the past-vs-future split inside that was a finer cut than 13 pages need. Tools stays separate: it's the one group where you assemble a hypothetical and evaluate it, a genuinely different action from looking. Also renamed: Estimator → Standings Estimator (states what it estimates), NBA Teams → NBA Depth Charts (states what the page is).

The rule — goes in the doc, not the nav

GroupAdmits a page when…Explicitly rejects
👥 Leagueit shows the current state of the 12 teams in your league League Analytics (time series) · Trade Analyzer (half free agents)
⛹️ Playersit shows the pool's or one player's recorded production Player Trends (time series) · Projections (model output)
📈 Insightsit's a trend over a time window, or a model's output — you look, you don't act Draft Report (single past event, no series) · Player Rankings (one weighted snapshot, no model) · Trade Analyzer (you assemble something, this group is look-only)
🛠️ Toolsyou assemble a move that doesn't exist yet and evaluate it Standings Estimator (output, nothing to assemble) · Projections (model output, nothing to assemble) · Player Rankings (weights, no hypothetical)
🏀 NBAit's real-NBA reference data, not scoped to your league anything roto-scored

Every rule has a stated rejection. That's what stops the next page from being dumped in — the old flat Tools drawer had no rejection list, which is how it ended up holding Trends and Draft Report.

What changes in code

delete icon-only tier 5 short group labels (League, Players, Insights, Tools, NBA) sum to 588px at the 768px md floor — fits without the text-xl xl:text-sm / hidden xl:inline compression the old 10-item nav needed. Full label shows from md straight through; one desktopItemClass, no dual icon-size branch. Confirm against real Tailwind metrics once wired up — mockup measurement has ~2px slack, not exact.
delete Tools config The current toolsItems array in Layout.tsx:165 (Projections, Estimator, Trade, Draft Report, Trends + Feature Store) is replaced. The dropdown component itself — hover, click, Escape, Arrow-Down, Tab-out, aria-haspopup/aria-expanded — is kept and made generic.
new group config One array of {label, icon, items[]} drives both desktop and mobile: League, Players, Insights, Tools, NBA. Flag gating (FF_PLAYER_RANKINGS, FF_PROJECTIONS, FF_DRAFT_REPORT, FF_TRENDS, FF_FEATURE_STORE) moves into each group's items.
refactor open state toolsOpen boolean → openGroup: string | null on desktop and mobileOpenGroup on mobile, so opening one group closes the others. Keyboard handlers keyed by group id.
rename nav labels Analytics → League Analytics, Trends → Player Trends, Estimator → Standings Estimator, the NBA child link → NBA Depth Charts (the group itself stays NBA). Nav labels only — routes and page headings untouched.
keep routes and flags Zero route changes. /shots keeps redirecting to /analytics. Whole reorg stays behind VITE_FF_NAV_REORG; off renders today's flat nav unchanged.
verify widths 5 group triggers icon-only between md and xl — fewer elements than today's nav, confirm at 768px. Mobile: 5 accordion rows, longest drawer 5, no height risk at 390px.
Still open: Tools ships holding one item until Streaming Planner or Slot Estimator lands. Either accept it as a placeholder for work already planned, or park Trade Analyzer in League and move it the day the second workspace ships. Flip the planned-pages toggle above to see it at three.
