Is this already covered?

no — genuinely new
Season grid (the existing table on this page) answers per team, per month. It cannot tell you what next Tuesday looks like.

Roster coverage (TeamDetail) is a 14-day bar strip too — but it counts your players with a game, not league games. That overlap is the one real design risk here: two 14-day bar strips in the same app that mean different things. Ⓐ leans into it (same visual language, so the reader transfers the reading) and mitigates by living on a different page with a different title. Ⓑ and Ⓒ sidestep it entirely by not being a bar strip.

Team schedule tab lists one team's games with a slate_size column — so the number already exists in the app, but only reachable one team at a time. Nothing anywhere lists who plays on a given date.

What it's for

a query, not advice
Light nights are where a roster spot earns or wastes itself: on a 5-game night most of your team is idle, so that is when an add matters and when a streamer either plays or doesn't. Heavy nights take care of themselves. The view states the slate; it ranks nobody and recommends nothing — same posture as the rest of the page.

Cost

frontend only
calendar_days[] is already built in schedule_service._build_payload and already shipped in the response — the frontend currently ignores it entirely. Matchups group client-side from the per-team games[] already in the same payload. So: no backend change, no extra request, no cache work. The only backend-adjacent question is whether the payload should pre-group matchups per date to save the client a 30×80 pass — it's ~2400 iterations once per load, so probably not worth the response-size increase.
Open question — what is "next 14 days" in the offseason? Today is Aug 2026; the season starts Oct 20. Anchor the window to max(today, season start) so the card shows opening fortnight rather than 14 empty cells, and label it honestly when it isn't actually "next".
Deliberately not here: your players. Overlaying "3 of your guys play" turns this into roster coverage, which already exists on TeamDetail. This card is league-wide and league-only. If you want them joined, the right move is a link, not a merge.
