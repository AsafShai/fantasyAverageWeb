1. Where does Trade go?

Players group Forecast group My League group
PlacementCase forCase against
Players You operate on players — pick names, compare production. Its second mode is your team vs free agents, which is waiver-wire scouting, the same job as browsing Players. A team-for-team trade is a league transaction, not player browsing.
Forecast It's a decision workspace, same moment as Streaming Planner and Slot Estimator — you're deciding a move. Breaks the predicate. Nothing in the page is model output, so the drawer stops meaning "model-driven" and starts meaning "stuff you decide with" — the vague kind of category that rotted Tools.
My League Trades happen between the 12 teams. Team A / Team B are league teams. Half the page is free agents, who aren't in your league at all. And My League otherwise holds current-state views, not workspaces.

Where I'd land: Players. The predicate settles Forecast (no model output, so no). Between Players and My League, Players wins on what you actually manipulate — you select players and compare their stats, exactly like the Players page, just two columns instead of one.
